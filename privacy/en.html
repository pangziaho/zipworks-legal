Privacy Policy

ZipWorks · Updated 3 October 2026

ZipWorks opens and creates archives on your device. Your files never leave your iPhone or iPad unless you send them somewhere yourself. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What the app accesses

  • Files you choose. When you import files from the Files app, Photos or another app, ZipWorks copies them into its own folder on your device (shown in the Files app under On My iPhone › ZipWorks). Archives are extracted and created there.
  • Photos. Picking photos uses the system picker, which gives the app only the items you select. The app asks for permission only to add to your photo library, when you choose to save compressed images or videos there.
  • Local network. Wi-Fi Transfer runs a small web server on your device only while its screen is open, so a computer on the same network can upload and download files directly. Nothing passes through our servers. The same permission lets the app reach WebDAV servers or a NAS on your local network.
  • WebDAV servers you add. The app connects only to the addresses you enter. User names are stored in the app and passwords in this device's keychain.
  • iCloud Drive (optional). If you turn on iCloud sync, files you put in the iCloud Drive location are stored in your iCloud Drive by Apple. We have no access to them.
  • Archive passwords. Passwords you choose to save are kept only in this device's keychain. They are not uploaded or synced.

What we do not do

  • No data is collected, transmitted to us or stored on our servers.
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No account, email or password is required.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Purchases

ZipWorks is a paid download sold by Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card, Apple ID or billing details.

Deleting your data

Delete files in the app or in the Files app. Settings › Clear Preview Cache removes temporary copies made for previews. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device; files in iCloud Drive stay there until you delete them.

Open-source software

Extraction and compression use open-source libraries (libarchive, UnRAR, LZMA SDK and others) that run entirely on your device and do not send data anywhere.

Children

ZipWorks is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
